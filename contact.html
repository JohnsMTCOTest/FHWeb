Planning tools

Finalize and Share Your Wishes

These templates help you capture service choices, disposition preferences, and personalization requests. Even if a funeral home is not involved, the forms below keep everyone aligned and aware of your options.

Downloadable checklists
  • Service selection worksheet (home funeral, funeral, memorial, graveside, burial at sea).
  • Disposition comparison: burial, green burial, cremation, alkaline hydrolysis, natural organic reduction.
  • Memorialization list: merchandise, experiential tributes, and family participation options.
  • Conflict check: embalming vs. green/NOR, cemetery vault rules, sea burial requirements.
Conversation starters
  • “Do we understand every option before choosing?”
  • “If we say ‘just cremation,’ what urn, container, and placement feels right?”
  • “Who should be present or participate in reef placement, scattering, or soil donation?”
  • “Which rituals are essential, and do any choices conflict with green burial or NOR rules?”

Steps to Put Your Plan Into Motion

Follow these steps to move from ideas to a clear, shareable plan.

Document

  • Write down preferred service format, disposition path, and memorialization choices.
  • List providers to contact (funeral director, hydrolysis provider, NOR facility, captain for sea burial).
  • Record who should be notified and who can make substitutions if something is unavailable.

Share

  • Give copies to your healthcare agent, executor, and close family or friends.
  • Store digital versions in an accessible location with passwords noted.
  • Revisit annually to update choices or providers.
Support

When to Call a Professional

While the guide is designed for self-directed planning, licensed providers can assist with permits, transportation, refrigeration, legal timelines, and hosting services. Use them when you want reassurance that requirements are met.

Questions to ask
  • Can family or friends witness the cremation, hydrolysis, or NOR placement?
  • May we accompany the remains to a reef or sea burial? Are there passenger limits?
  • What containers, shrouds, or urns are approved for your equipment and local regulations?
  • How do you handle conflicts like embalming requests before a planned green burial?
