Memorialization

Create Keepsakes and Experiences

Use the options below to personalize the tribute, even if a funeral home is not involved. Many items pair well with cremation, hydrolysis, burial, or natural organic reduction. Ask providers about participation (family ride-alongs, pouring ashes into reef molds, or attending soil donations).

Merchandise

Tangible Memorial Pieces

Select one or combine a few to reflect hobbies, spirituality, or environmental values.

Mushroom Suit

Biodegradable suit embedded with mushroom spores to support natural decomposition and soil health.

Tree Pods & Planters

Biodegradable pods that hold cremated remains or NOR soil with a tree seedling for living memorials.

Art Glass & Jewelry

Small portions of cremated remains or hydrolysis powder fused into glass or wearable pieces.

Vinyl Records

Press ashes into custom records with favorite songs, spoken stories, or guided meditations.

Eternal Reef Kits

Mix cremated remains into a reef mold that is later placed at sea as marine habitat.

Dispersing Walking Stick

Walking stick with a chamber for controlled scattering along favorite trails.

Canvas or Paintings

Artists can incorporate cremated remains into paint for a custom artwork or portrait.

Urn Variety

Wood, metal, ceramic, biodegradable paper/salt, water-soluble urns, or keepsake minis for sharing.

Services & Experiences

Memorial Events and Destinations

Blend these ideas with any disposition choice to make the farewell memorable.

Experiences
  • Space flight: send a symbolic portion of remains on a memorial flight.
  • Eternal reefs: family can assist with the pour and accompany the reef placement when policies allow.
  • Sea scattering: sail with a licensed captain; combine with readings, flowers, and biodegradable urns.
  • Soil donation: gift NOR soil to a forest, garden, or conservation project.
Service touches
  • Memory tables with favorite items, letters, or recipes.
  • Guest participation: speak, sing, paint, or place soil/flowers during the ceremony.
  • Livestreaming for remote guests or recording stories for a digital archive.
  • Combining rituals: viewing before a green burial, or a memorial after NOR soil is ready.
Participation

Ways Family and Friends Can Be Involved

Clarify expectations early to honor cultural, spiritual, and personal boundaries.

Hands-on options
  • Accompany remains to a reef deployment or burial at sea (when vessel policies allow).
  • Carry or lower the casket, shroud, urn, or soil during graveside or green burials.
  • Pour ashes into reef molds or biodegradable urns; place soil at a tree planting.
Personal touches
  • Write messages on the container, urn, or reef mold.
  • Create playlists, photo loops, or art stations for guests to contribute.
  • Invite a facilitator to help balance storytelling with quiet reflection.
