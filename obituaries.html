Memorialization

Design a tribute that matches the person and the plan

Pair merchandise with experiences and participation guidelines so every choice supports the selected disposition.

Merchandise

Tangible keepsakes and eco-forward options

Use these ideas to spark conversation about what feels fitting and what aligns with your disposition choice.

Biodegradable

Mushroom suit, water-soluble or paper urns, salt urns for water scattering, and biodegradable NOR soil bags.

Living memorials

Tree pods, seed paper programs, planters for NOR soil portions, and garden markers with GPS coordinates.

Artistic keepsakes

Art glass orbs, jewelry, vinyl records, paintings incorporating cremated remains, or custom ceramics.

Display items

Engraved urns, photo plates, memory boxes, and story tables for services or home display.

Experiences

Send-offs that emphasize place and participation

Match the experience to the chosen disposition, then note who will attend and what permissions are needed.

Experiential ideas
  • Eternal reefs with family present for pouring and placement, when allowed.
  • Space flight memorials, balloon or drone-assisted scatterings (where legal), or guided mountain hikes.
  • Soil donations from NOR to conservation forests or community gardens.
Participation rules
  • Confirm who can accompany remains to a reef vessel or facility; list names in the workbook.
  • Check passenger limits, mobility needs, and weather policies for boats or outdoor venues.
  • Assign roles for reading, music, or pouring ashes/soil so everyone knows what to expect.
Compatibility

Keep tributes aligned with disposition rules

Use these checks to ensure your chosen keepsakes and experiences fit the legal and environmental requirements.

Checks for burial & green burial
  • Confirm cemetery rules for markers, vaults, biodegradable materials, and planting permissions.
  • For green burial, avoid embalming and select untreated wood, wicker, or shroud-only burial.
Checks for cremation, hydrolysis, and NOR
  • Use urns or soil containers that meet scattering or burial requirements; verify water-scattering rules.
  • Align art glass or vinyl projects with the amount of remains available; keep a small portion for backup.
  • For NOR, confirm soil transport rules and donation acceptance before planning ceremonies.
