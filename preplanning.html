Plan Today for Tomorrow's Peace of Mind

Preplanning empowers you to share your story the way you wish, while relieving loved ones of difficult decisions during an emotional time.

Why Plan Ahead

Benefits for You and Your Family

Provide Clarity

Document your service preferences, music selections, obituary details, and disposition choices. Your loved ones gain peace of mind knowing they are honoring your wishes.

Ease Financial Stress

Explore secure funding options with flexible payments and price protection. We partner with trusted insurance and pre-need providers to safeguard your investment.

We're Here to Help

Consultations: Complimentary appointments in-person, virtual, or by phone.

Resources: Planning checklist, vital documents guide, and obituary worksheet.

Support: Multilingual staff and notary services available upon request.

How It Works

Three Simple Steps

1. Conversation

Meet with our preplanning specialist to discuss your story, family traditions, and desired level of personalization.

2. Decisions

Select service options, merchandise, and ceremony details. We'll outline costs and funding choices in a clear, itemized plan.

3. Confidence

Receive copies of your arrangements to share with loved ones. We securely store your wishes and confirm them annually.

Planning Checklist

Gather Important Information

  • Full legal name, date of birth, and Social Security number
  • Military service details and DD-214 (if applicable)
  • Emergency contacts and next of kin information
  • Favorite readings, songs, hobbies, and personal stories
  • Preferred charities for memorial donations
