Decision support

"We just want a cremation."

Even a simple cremation includes choices: type of container, witness options, urn style, and where cremated remains are placed (home, burial, scattering, reef, or turned into glass or records). Explore the full picture first so the plan matches your values.

  • Understand costs and what is included in each pathway.
  • Check for conflicts (for example, embalming is not permitted for green burial or NOR).
  • Document who should be present and what rituals matter most.
