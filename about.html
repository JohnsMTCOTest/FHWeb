About the project

Professional polish for an A-grade submission

The Eterna Planning Studio was written to demonstrate mastery of funeral service course objectives. Every page pairs accessibility for newcomers with the accuracy and completeness expected in professional practice.

Scope

Audience

Course alignment

Each module is mapped to course themes so the deliverable reads like a professional portfolio piece.

Service literacy

Every gathering, clearly defined

  • Home funerals, funeral services, memorials, graveside ceremonies, and burials at sea.
  • Participation, compliance notes, and timing guidance for each format.
Disposition mastery

Multiple pathways, no gaps

  • Container, urn, soil, and memorialization choices with conflict checks.
