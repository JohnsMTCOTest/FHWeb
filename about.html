Our Story & Our Promise

Family owned and operated for more than six decades, Johnson Funeral Home & Crematory is built on a legacy of compassion, integrity, and community-centered service.

Committed to the Families We Serve

Since opening our doors in 1961, we have believed that every life deserves to be celebrated with grace and authenticity. Our licensed funeral directors and support team devote themselves to guiding families through planning with clarity and kindness.

Today, Johnson Funeral Home & Crematory provides full-service funeral care, from traditional services to innovative celebrations of life. With an on-site crematory, arrangement suites, and reception spaces, we offer everything needed to honor a life in one peaceful location.

We are proud to support veterans, first responders, and multi-generational families throughout the River Valley. Giving back through scholarships, grief programs, and community partnerships remains central to our mission.

Guiding Principles

  • Serve every family with dignity, respect, and cultural sensitivity.
  • Provide transparent options and pricing to support informed decisions.
  • Honor personal stories through meaningful, customized memorials.
  • Extend care beyond the service with grief and healing resources.
Aftercare & Support

Continuing Care for Your Journey

Healing continues long after the service concludes. We partner with licensed counselors, host monthly support gatherings, and offer online grief libraries to help families navigate loss at their own pace.

Our staff can assist with completing insurance documents, Social Security benefits, and veteran honors. We also coordinate memorial anniversaries and tribute events, ensuring your loved one's legacy continues to be celebrated.
